where do the mountains come from?

every mountain is calculated from scratch: the peaks, the folds in the rock, and the pencil strokes. each new landscape starts with a fresh random seed. the studio draws everything in your browser, without using the existing mountain images.

choose a foreground terrain style, then give the middle and distant layers their own styles or let them inherit the foreground. creativity explores focal peaks, quieter gaps, and balanced contrasts while keeping the pencil strokes. save a landscape link to return to that exact drawing and its settings.

your landscape details will appear here when the drawing is ready.
